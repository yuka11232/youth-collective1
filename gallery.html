Business / CommunityNational2026

Marsol Expo · Local Enterprise Briefing

Marsol Expo · June 2026

Members observed exhibitions, conducted interviews, and produced a concise briefing on local enterprise themes.

  • GoalUnderstand how local companies describe their work and community value.
  • Signature exerciseA "pitch-versus-proof" audit — checking founders' spoken narratives against their own production numbers and pricing claims, using the cross-examination discipline of the business-school case method.
  • OutcomeA short briefing and follow-up questions.
Energy / InnovationInternational2026

Azerbaijan Energy Week · Sustainability Briefing

Baku · June 2026

Members used the event to gather questions, interview presenters, and develop a short synthesis on sustainability and innovation.

  • GoalExplore public-facing energy ideas with a youth lens.
  • Signature exerciseA shadow rebuttal panel, modeled on Model UN cross-examination, stress-testing Global North transition policy — carbon pricing, subsidy phase-outs — against the realities of a hydrocarbon-reliant economy.
  • OutcomeA research brief and discussion outline.
ForumsInternational2026

WUF13 · Urban Futures Briefing

World Urban Forum · 2026

Youth Collective documented public-space and governance themes from forum sessions while preparing a compact briefing and a set of original presentations on inclusion and urban technology.

  • GoalTranslate urban forum discussion into clear youth-facing analysis.
  • Signature exerciseOriginal presentations and speeches on how smart-city technology and urban design decisions shape digital exclusion for expat and minority residents, framed around the urbanist idea of the "right to the city."
  • OutcomeSession notes and debate prompts.
CommunityLocal2026

Baku Youth Ideas Lab

Baku · July 2026

Using John Locke Essay Competition questions, the program made philosophy, science, and public policy accessible through imagination, debate, and ethical AI-supported reasoning. The gallery draws directly from the John Locke photo folder to document the workshop atmosphere and student engagement.

  • GoalStrengthen original reasoning and debate skills.
  • Signature exerciseReading the philosopher's own theory of natural rights and consent against modern algorithmic governance, to test whether 18th-century social-contract logic holds up against systems nobody explicitly consented to.
  • OutcomeArgument outlines, reflection notes, and counterargument prompts.
